Q1 Physics Medium

A particle moves along a straight line with its position given by x = t³ - 6t² + 9t + 5, where x is in meters and t is in seconds. What is the acceleration of the particle when the velocity is zero?

Explanation: First find velocity by differentiating position: v = dx/dt = 3t² - 12t + 9. Set v = 0 to find when velocity is zero: 3t² - 12t + 9 = 0 → t² - 4t + 3 = 0 → t = 1s or t = 3s. Acceleration is a = dv/dt = 6t - 12. At t=1s: a = -6 m/s², at t=3s: a = 6 m/s².

Correct Answer: B

Q2 Chemistry Hard

Which of the following compounds will show geometrical isomerism?

Explanation: Geometrical isomerism occurs in alkenes when each double bonded carbon is attached to two different groups. 2-Butene (CH₃-CH=CH-CH₃) can exist as cis and trans forms, while the other options don't have two different groups on both carbons of the double bond.

Correct Answer: B
